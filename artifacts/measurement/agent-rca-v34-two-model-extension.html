What we found

  1. Raw reduced input for some models. [confirmed]
    • gemini-3.8-flash: Raw used fewer input tokens (10/11 eligible cases; median delta -1,575,917).
  2. Graph has not shown a general end-to-end efficiency gain. [not_confirmed]
    • No efficiency endpoint passed Holm correction. The small sample does not establish equivalence.
  3. Graph diagnosis results differ across fault types. [descriptive]
    • Service/dependency faults (OpenRCA2 ops-lite): Graph 40, Raw 39 correct diagnoses
    • Node faults (RCA-100 v1.1): Graph 7, Raw 11 correct diagnoses
    • Post-measurement comparison: fault type and dataset are confounded, so their effects cannot be separated.
